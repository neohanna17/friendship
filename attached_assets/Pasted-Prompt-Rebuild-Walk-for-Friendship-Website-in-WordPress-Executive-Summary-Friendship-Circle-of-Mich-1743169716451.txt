Prompt: Rebuild Walk for Friendship Website in WordPress
Executive Summary
Friendship Circle of Michigan is seeking to transition its Walk for Friendship fundraising website from Rallybound (Neon One) to a fully customized, modern, and more flexible WordPress-based platform. This rebuild should replicate all current functionality, enhance the user experience, reduce long-term costs, and allow for easy staff management.

🔧 Project Scope: Walk for Friendship Website Redesign
🔹 Platform Goals
Migrate the site from Rallybound to WordPress

Maintain existing branding and colors

Make it more dynamic, fun, and engaging

Improve mobile experience and site performance

Ensure the new site is self-manageable by non-technical staff

🧩 Required Features
🔸 Peer-to-Peer Fundraising
Team Registration: Ability for users to create teams and invite members

Team & Individual Fundraising Pages: Each with editable bios, photos, and donation progress

Hierarchical Structure: Teams → Captains → Members

Sponsorship Options: Tiered sponsorship packages with recognition

Leaderboards: Highlighting top teams and individuals

Fundraising Thermometers: Real-time goal tracking

🔸 Payment & Donation Options
Stripe integration as primary processor (with support for PayPal, Venmo, etc.)

Track processing fees by method

Recurring donations

Tribute donations (In honor/memory)

Upsell prompts to boost donations

🔄 Data & CRM Integration
Salesforce Integration is critical

Sync donor data, transactions, teams, campaigns, and custom fields

Full API access for customization and reporting

Zapier-compatible triggers for marketing and automation

📈 Back-End Admin Requirements
Staff-friendly dashboard to view/manage:

All fundraisers, donors, and teams

Fundraising stats and reports

Donation history and attribution

Campaign Cloning: Easily replicate campaigns annually

Set and automate fundraising milestone notifications (25%, 50%, 75%)

💌 Marketing & Engagement
Mailchimp Integration for drip campaigns

Automated Emails for registration, milestones, and thank-yous

Social sharing for individual/team fundraising pages

🎨 Design Requirements
Keep Friendship Circle’s brand colors and style

Add animation, interactivity, or micro-interactions to make it more fun

Improve site speed and responsiveness

Use a clean, accessible design for all users

📅 Timeline
Walk site must be ready by May 2025

Fundraising starts July 1, 2025

Walk event in August 2025

📦 Deliverables
Fully functional WordPress site (with Elementor or Gutenberg preferred)

Admin documentation/training for staff

Salesforce integration working and tested

All legacy functionality from Rallybound replicated or improved

Ongoing support optional but preferred

🧠 Notes for Dev Team
You will not be rebuilding the Great Bike Giveaway site (separate project)

You can reuse elements from Rallybound for inspiration, but we want a more modern, interactive, and joyful feel for the new version

Please suggest creative ways to make the site more engaging for families and donors
