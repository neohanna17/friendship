 2. Walk for Friendship: A more traditional peer-to-peer fundraising walk event, raising close to $1 million annually.

Core Functionality
Team Registration: Standard team creation and management
Team Captain/Member Structure: Hierarchy for team management
Fundraising Pages: For teams and individuals
Sponsorship Options: Various sponsorship levels and recognition
Reporting: Comprehensive reporting on teams, donations, etc.
Data & Integration Requirements
Salesforce Integration: Critical requirement for all donation data
Data Points Needed:
Donor information (contact details)
Transaction details (amount, date, payment method)
Campaign association
Team/individual attribution
Custom fields for reporting
Payment Processing:
Currently using multiple processors (Stripe, Authorize.net, PayPal, Venmo)
Preference for Stripe due to better API
Need to track different processing fees by payment method
Administrative Requirements
Back-End Access: Staff needs easy access to:
Lists of teams
Lists of fundraisers
Lists of donors
Donor details by fundraiser
Campaign statistics and reports
Campaign Replication: Ability to easily duplicate campaigns year-to-year
Self-Management: Platform should be manageable by non-technical staff
Additional Requirements
Tributes: Ability to make tribute donations
Recurring Donations: Support for monthly/sustaining donors
Upselling: Prompts to encourage recurring donations or increase one-time amounts
Current Pain Points
Lack of Innovation: Rallybound hasn't significantly updated features in 5+ years
Integration Issues: Occasional sync failures with Salesforce
Support Challenges: Slow response times and difficulty resolving issues
Limited Customization: Required custom development for new payment methods
High Costs: 3% platform fee considered excessive
Timeline Considerations
Walk for Friendship site needed by May 2025
Fundraising begins July 1, 2025
Walk event August 2025
Great Bike Giveaway planning would need to begin immediately for November 2025 launch
Budget Implications
Current spending: approximately $18,000/year on Great Bike Giveaway platform fees
Additional fees for Walk for Friendship and other donation forms
Development costs would need to be weighed against long-term savings
Decision Factors
Ability to replicate unique voting mechanism
Ease of Salesforce integration
Total cost (platform fees vs. development)
Timeline for implementation
Self-service capability for staff
Innovation potential and roadmap
